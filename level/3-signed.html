AI Accountability Scale

How much responsibility an author takes for a document written with LLM tools 		Scale version v1.0
Author Peter Kutsos

The AI Accountability Scale is a system meant to help teams bring LLM tools into their everyday collaboration. By placing a document on the scale, the author gives an honest assessment of the effort they put into it and communicates that assessment to colleagues.

Recipients can then fairly decide which parts of the document to give feedback on and where to expect changes from the author first.

3 SIGNEDI take responsibility for the layout, the structure and every sentence. I sign my name to this document and consider it finished.

The author of the document you came from committed to this level by answering YES to these questions:

  1. Have you read all of it?
  2. Is the direction of the document relevant?
  3. Do you understand every sentence and can you put it in your own words?
  4. Do you know where every number comes from?
  5. Do you agree with every claim?
  6. Do you agree with every conclusion? Do the conclusions follow from the claims?
  7. Is every sentence worded the way you would word it yourself?
  8. Is the structure of the document logical and clear?
  9. Is the document's appearance presentable?
