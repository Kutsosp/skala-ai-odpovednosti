AI Accountability Scale

How much responsibility an author takes for a document written with LLM tools 		Version v1.0
Updated 30 Sep 2026
Author Peter Kutsos Status 1 DRAFT

1 DRAFT The direction is relevant. I understand every sentence and can put it in my own words. Please respond to the direction before I start refining the details. This is a first translation of the Czech original; the wording has not been reviewed yet.

The AI Accountability Scale is a system meant to help teams bring LLM tools into their everyday collaboration. By placing a document on the scale, the author gives an honest assessment of the effort they put into it and communicates that assessment to colleagues.

Recipients can then fairly decide which parts of the document to give feedback on and where to expect changes from the author first.

Purpose

  • Make clear what kind of feedback the author and the work are ready for (a rough draft can be criticised for its overall idea, but not yet for its style)
  • Prevent information cascades
  • Keep responsibility with the author
  • Communicate the effort the author put into the work, efficiently and honestly
  • Let the author share early versions and get feedback on them without wasting time or provoking needlessly negative reactions
  • Give the reader an idea of what to focus on in their feedback and what the author will still finish

The four levels

Write one of them in capitals on the first line of the document, e-mail or message:

# Level What I am responsible for When to use
0 FORWARDED I take no responsibility for the content. I made no substantial changes, or I haven't read it. Use when the recipient has no access to LLM tools; otherwise they could have generated it themselves.
1 DRAFT The direction is relevant. I understand every sentence and can put it in my own words. Please respond to the direction before I start refining the details.
2 VERIFIED I know where every number comes from and I agree with every claim and conclusion. Please respond to the claims and arguments before I start refining the wording, layout and structure.
3 SIGNED I take responsibility for the layout, the structure and every sentence. I sign my name to this document and consider it finished.

Which level to commit to?

To move up a level, you must answer YES to every question.

Answering YES means the author is responsible for that aspect of the work and accepts feedback and criticism on it.

0 FORWARDED → 1 DRAFT



1 DRAFT → 2 VERIFIED



2 VERIFIED → 3 SIGNED



Since AI cannot be held responsible, there is no partial responsibility for the author. If, for example, the author knows where only some of the numbers come from, the document cannot move to VERIFIED.

Badge

Answer the questions above and copy the level badge into your document, e-mail or message. The Chrome extension does the same, and in Google Docs, Sheets and Slides it inserts the badge directly into the document.
